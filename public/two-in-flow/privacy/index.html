Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Two in Flow handles information when you use the game.

1. Information we collect

Two in Flow does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. Game preferences and statistics are stored locally on your device.

2. Analytics, advertising and purchases

The current version has no analytics, advertising, in-app purchases, or network services. This policy will be updated before any of those features are added.

3. Children’s privacy

Two in Flow is designed to be family-friendly. Because the game does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The current version will always be published on this page with its updated date.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact appalapas@gmail.com.
