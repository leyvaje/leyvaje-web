FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common questions.

Everything in NotedTS runs locally on your device. Here is how to get the best experience on iOS and Android.

How do I download the speech-to-text and summary models (Wi-Fi vs. mobile data)?

Open Settings → On-device models inside NotedTS:

  • Multilingual speech-to-text model (Whisper ggml-base.bin, ~141 MB)
  • Conversation summary model (Qwen3 Qwen3-0.6B-Q8_0.gguf, ~610 MB)

Tap Download next to each model. Downloads use Wi-Fi by default and pause automatically if Wi-Fi disconnects. If Wi-Fi is unavailable, NotedTS asks "Use mobile data?" before using cellular data. If a download is interrupted, tap Resume to continue from where it stopped. Each file is verified with a SHA-256 checksum before activation.

Why does processing run in the foreground and keep the screen awake?

On-device speech-to-text (Whisper) and LLM summarization (Qwen3) are compute-intensive native tasks. On iOS and Android, mobile operating systems suspend or terminate heavy background CPU/GPU work to protect battery life.

To prevent your transcription or summary from being cut off by the OS, NotedTS processes recordings while the app is open in the foreground and temporarily keeps your screen awake (wakelock) until the active job finishes. Once processing completes, normal screen auto-lock is restored immediately.

How do I pause, resume, or retry failed summaries ("Process again" / "Retry all")?
  • Automatic pause & resume: If you start a new recording while a note is being processed, or if you switch away from the app, NotedTS safely pauses the processing queue and resumes when you finish recording or return to the app.
  • Retry a single conversation: If transcription or summarization fails or is interrupted (for example, if a model was not yet installed), tap Process again on that conversation card in your notes list.
  • Retry all failed summaries: When one or more transcribed notes have a failed summary, a banner appears at the top of the home screen showing how many summaries failed. Tap Retry all to queue and re-run summarization for all of them in order.
What happens during long recordings (60-minute notice & 2-hour automatic safety stop)?

To protect device storage, memory, and battery while ensuring long conversations are never lost:

  • At 60 minutes: A soft inline notice appears in the recording panel ("Long recording · Auto-saves at 2 hours") while recording continues uninterrupted.
  • At 2 hours: The recording stops automatically, saves the audio file to your device, and displays a confirmation banner ("Recording stopped automatically at 2 hours for safety and was saved."). You can immediately start a new recording if your meeting continues.
How do I delete audio files while keeping my transcripts and summaries?

Raw 16 kHz mono WAV audio files take up the most disk space, whereas text transcripts and summaries use very little storage. Once a recording has been transcribed, you can delete its audio file in two ways:

  • Single conversation: Open the conversation detail screen and tap the delete audio action on the audio player card, or open Settings → Recording storage and tap the trash icon next to that recording.
  • All transcribed recordings at once: Open Settings → Recording storage and tap Delete all in the top bar. Any recording that is still awaiting transcription (marked PENDING) is kept safe until its transcript is complete.
Can I delete or replace the downloaded AI models to free up space?

Yes. Go to Settings → On-device models, tap the menu button next to an installed model, and choose Delete model (or Replace model to re-download and re-verify it). Deleting a model does not delete your existing transcripts or summaries.
