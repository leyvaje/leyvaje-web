Key features

Built for privacy-first conversation notes.

Everything runs locally on your device after a one-time model download in Settings.

1

Multilingual on-device transcription

Speech-to-text runs locally using OpenAI Whisper (ggml-base.bin via whisper.cpp), transcribing conversations in their original language without sending audio to external servers.

2

Source-language summaries & action items

On-device summarization powered by Qwen3 (Qwen3-0.6B-Q8_0.gguf via llama.cpp) produces a brief summary, key points, and source-supported action items in the same language as your transcript.

3

Delete audio, keep your notes

Once a recording is transcribed, you can delete its 16 kHz mono WAV audio file individually or in bulk from Storage while keeping the transcript and summary on your device.

4

Verified one-time model downloads

Download the speech-to-text (~141 MB) and summary (~610 MB) models once over HTTPS from Hugging Face (Wi-Fi by default, or mobile data with your approval), verified with SHA-256 checksums before use.

5

Recording safety limits

Recordings show a soft inline notice at 60 minutes ("Long recording · Auto-saves at 2 hours") and automatically stop and save at 2 hours so long sessions are never lost.

6

No cloud, accounts, or telemetry

NotedTS requires no sign-in and includes no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no cloud sync. Conversations stay local to your installation until you delete them.

How it works

From spoken conversation to structured notes.

Always inform everyone in the conversation and obtain any consent required by local law before you start recording.

01

Download models once

Open Settings → On-device models to download the multilingual speech-to-text model and the conversation summary model over Wi-Fi (or mobile data when you approve).

02

Record with consent

Tap Record to capture 16 kHz mono WAV audio locally. On Android, a microphone foreground service notification remains visible while recording.

03

Review, share & manage storage

Keep the app open while foreground transcription and summarization complete. Read or share your transcript and summary, and delete the raw audio file whenever you want to free space.
