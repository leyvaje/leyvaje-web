Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

Where does ChillBill store my data?

Invoices, insurance profiles, settings and added photos are stored locally on your device. ChillBill does not require a user account.

Are my data automatically sent to an insurer?

No. ChillBill does not transmit invoices or insurance data to insurers or other external parties.

Is the reimbursement estimate binding?

No. Results are based on your entries and are for guidance only. Check important decisions with your insurer or a qualified professional.

What happens when I delete the app?

Because the app stores its data locally, app data can be lost when the app is deleted. Keep important original documents elsewhere before deleting it.

Why does ChillBill ask for camera or photo access?

Only so you can attach a photo to an invoice if you choose. You can also use ChillBill without invoice photos.

How do I find my app version?

Open the “About ChillBill” section in the app. Your installed version is shown at the bottom of the page.
