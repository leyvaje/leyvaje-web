2. Principle

ChillBill processes your invoice and insurance data locally on your device. There is no ChillBill account, no cloud synchronisation, no advertising, no usage analytics and no tracking by the app.

3. App data stored locally

Depending on how you use the app, ChillBill may store the following information locally:

  • insurance profiles and annual insurance settings,
  • invoice amounts, dates, categories, practice names and notes,
  • deductible, bonus and contribution-refund information,
  • invoice photos you select or take, and
  • app settings.

ChillBill does not transmit these data to us or to an insurer. They remain in the app’s local storage until you remove them in the app or delete the app and its data.

4. Device permissions

Only after your explicit choice and iOS permission, ChillBill can access these device features:

  • Camera: to take a new invoice photo,
  • Photo library: to choose an existing image,
  • Calendar or Reminders: to send a selected invoice due date to the corresponding system app.

You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings. ChillBill can be used without invoice photos.

5. No analytics or automated decisions

The app uses no analytics, advertising or tracking services and creates no user profiles. Potential reimbursement calculations happen locally from your entries. They are non-binding guidance only and are not automated decisions with legal effect.

6. External links and PayPal

If you voluntarily open the PayPal support link in the app, you leave ChillBill. Further processing is governed by PayPal’s privacy policy. ChillBill does not receive payment information, and there are no in-app payments.

7. Email support

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and the content you provide to answer your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in handling support enquiries. Messages are deleted when they are no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Please do not send invoices, diagnoses or other health data by email.

8. Website hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel. When you visit it, technically necessary connection data such as IP address, time of access, requested page, browser and device information may be processed in server logs. This is necessary to deliver the website securely and reliably. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The ChillBill pages do not use their own analytics or advertising cookies and do not load external fonts or marketing content.

9. Your rights

Where we process your personal data, you have the rights provided by law, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

We cannot access app data stored locally in ChillBill. You manage and delete them directly in the app or on your device.

10. Changes

We update this privacy policy when functions, external services or legal requirements change. The current version is available at this URL.
